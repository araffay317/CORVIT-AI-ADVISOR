Paid Courses, Fees, Payment Methods, Installments

SOURCE:

IMPORTANT:
Fees and course availability can change according to course, batch,
campus, timing, and current offers. The chatbot must NOT invent or
guess a fee.

--------------------------------------------------
2. COURSE FEE
--------------------------------------------------

Corvit's website provides course/training information, but a complete
public and permanently fixed numeric fee list is not available for
every course.

Therefore:

- Do not assume that every course has the same fee.
- Do not provide an old fee as the current fee.
- Do not make up a fee if the exact current fee is unavailable.
- Fee can depend on the selected course and current batch.
- Students should verify the latest fee directly with Corvit before
  admission/payment.

Recommended chatbot response when exact fee is unavailable:

"The fee depends on the selected course and current batch. Corvit's
latest fee should be confirmed with the campus/admissions team. I can
help you identify the course, duration, timetable and admission
requirements first."

--------------------------------------------------
3. PAYMENT METHOD
--------------------------------------------------

According to Corvit's FAQ:

For in-campus students:

- Payment can be made through cash or cheque.

For online students:

- Payment can be made through the available online payment methods,
  including bank/payment arrangements mentioned by Corvit.

Students should confirm the currently accepted payment method before
making payment.

--------------------------------------------------
4. INSTALLMENT FACILITY
--------------------------------------------------

Corvit's FAQ states that:

- Course fee is generally payable at the beginning of the course.
- In certain cases, deserving students may be allowed to pay the fee
  in two equal installments.
- Installment availability should be confirmed with Corvit.

The chatbot must NOT promise installments to every student.

Recommended chatbot response:

"Corvit may allow two equal installments for certain deserving
students. Please confirm your eligibility with the Corvit campus
before admission."

--------------------------------------------------
5. CORPORATE TRAINING PAYMENT
--------------------------------------------------

For corporate training, Corvit states that payment is generally
required in advance.

The chatbot should distinguish corporate training from normal
individual student admission.

--------------------------------------------------
6. ONLINE VS ON-CAMPUS TRAINING
--------------------------------------------------

Corvit provides both physical/on-campus and online/virtual training
for different programs.

Availability depends on the specific course and current batch.

The chatbot should always check the latest course/batch information
before claiming that a particular course is currently available
online or on campus.

--------------------------------------------------
7. PAID VS NAVTTC
--------------------------------------------------

PAID TRAINING:

- Student pays the course/training fee.
- Fee depends on course and current batch.
- Payment rules depend on Corvit's current policy.
- Installments may be available in certain cases.

NAVTTC TRAINING:

- NAVTTC programs advertised by Corvit may be government-funded/free
  for eligible students.
- Eligibility requirements apply.
- Seats may be limited.
- Admission depends on the current NAVTTC program and selection
  process.

IMPORTANT:
A course appearing in Corvit's normal course list does NOT
automatically mean that it is currently available as a free NAVTTC
course.

--------------------------------------------------
8. FEE-RELATED CHATBOT RULES
--------------------------------------------------

If a student asks:

"Fee kitni hai?"

The chatbot should first identify:

1. Course name
2. Campus/online preference
3. Current batch if known

If the exact current fee is not available in the dataset:

"The current fee depends on the course and batch. Please confirm the
latest fee with Corvit admissions."

Never invent a numeric fee.

If a student asks:

"Installment ho sakti hai?"

Answer:

"Corvit's FAQ mentions that certain deserving students may be allowed
to pay in two equal installments. Please confirm with Corvit before
enrollment."

If a student asks:

"Kya ye course free hai?"

The chatbot should determine whether the student is asking about:

- Normal paid Corvit training
OR
- A current NAVTTC-funded program.

Do not call a normal paid course free unless a current official source
confirms it.

--------------------------------------------------
9. FEE ACCURACY POLICY
--------------------------------------------------

The chatbot must follow these rules:

- Never guess a course fee.
- Never use an outdated fee as the current fee.
- Never guarantee a discount.
- Never guarantee installment approval.
- Never claim a course is free without official confirmation.
- Clearly tell the student when fee information needs verification.
- Prioritize current official Corvit information.

--------------------------------------------------
10. GENERAL STUDENT GUIDANCE
--------------------------------------------------

If the student has not selected a course yet, the chatbot should
recommend a suitable course based on:

- Student's interests
- Previous education/background
- Programming interest
- Career goal
- Beginner/intermediate/advanced level
- Preferred learning area

After recommending a course, the chatbot can provide:

- Course outline
- Duration
- Timetable
- Training mode
- Fee information if currently available
- Admission/application information
- Infrastructure information

--------------------------------------------------
11. IMPORTANT CURRENT-DATA RULE
--------------------------------------------------

Timetable, fees, admission dates, discounts, course availability and
NAVTTC batches are time-sensitive.

The chatbot should treat these as dynamic information and should
prefer the latest official Corvit source whenever available.
