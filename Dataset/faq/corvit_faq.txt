--------------------------------------------------

Not all Corvit courses are free.

11. WHAT IS NAVTTC?
--------------------------------------------------

NAVTTC stands for National Vocational and Technical Training
Commission.

Corvit advertises certain NAVTTC-supported training programs.

Eligible students may be able to receive training under
government-funded programs.

Availability and eligibility depend on the current NAVTTC program.

13. HOW DO I APPLY FOR A COURSE?
--------------------------------------------------

General process:

1. Select a course.
2. Check course availability.
3. Check timetable.
4. Check eligibility if applicable.
5. Check current fee if it is a paid course.
6. Submit enrollment/application details.
7. Complete the applicable payment process.
8. Follow Corvit's admission instructions.
