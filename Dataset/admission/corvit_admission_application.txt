CORVIT SYSTEMS — ADMISSION & APPLICATION DATA
==================================================

DATA TYPE:
Admission Process, Enrollment, Application, Required Information

SOURCE:
Official Corvit Systems website

--------------------------------------------------
1. HOW TO APPLY
--------------------------------------------------

Students can enroll in Corvit courses through the official enrollment
process.

The official enrollment form asks for information such as:

- Student name
- Course selection
- Phone number
- Email address
- Institute/campus selection

Students should select the course they want to join and provide
correct contact information.

--------------------------------------------------
2. GENERAL ADMISSION PROCESS
--------------------------------------------------

Recommended admission flow:

Step 1:
Choose the desired course.

Step 2:
Check whether the course is currently being offered.

Step 3:
Check the latest timetable and available batch.

Step 4:
Check the current course fee if it is a paid course.

Step 5:
Check eligibility requirements where applicable.

Step 6:
Submit the enrollment/application information.

Step 7:
Corvit admissions/campus staff can provide further information
about enrollment, fee payment and class commencement.

--------------------------------------------------
3. INFORMATION STUDENT SHOULD PROVIDE
--------------------------------------------------

A student may be asked for:

- Full name
- Phone number
- Email address
- Selected course
- Preferred institute/campus

Additional documents may be requested depending on the course,
campus or program.

--------------------------------------------------
4. NAVTTC ADMISSION
--------------------------------------------------

NAVTTC-funded programs have their own eligibility and selection
requirements.

For NAVTTC programs:

- Student must meet the applicable eligibility requirements.
- Registration/application must be completed according to the
  current NAVTTC/Corvit process.
- Required documents may need to be submitted.
- Test/interview may be required depending on the program.
- Seats can be limited.
- Selection is subject to the current program rules.

The chatbot must not guarantee NAVTTC admission.

--------------------------------------------------
5. PAID COURSE ADMISSION
--------------------------------------------------

For normal paid courses:

- Student selects a course.
- Student checks the current batch/timetable.
- Student confirms the current fee.
- Student submits enrollment information.
- Student completes the applicable payment process.
- Student receives further instructions from Corvit.

Exact admission requirements can vary by course.

7. RAWALPINDI ENROLLMENT EXAMPLE
--------------------------------------------------

Corvit's Rawalpindi enrollment information provides a simple
three-step process:

Step 1 — Contact Corvit:
Call or WhatsApp to confirm course/seat availability.

Step 2 — Submit Documents:
The published Rawalpindi enrollment information mentions documents
such as:

- 2 copies of degree
- CV
- 2 passport-size photographs
- CNIC

Students should confirm the latest document requirements before
visiting the campus.

Step 3 — Attend Classes:
Students can attend according to the selected course's available
delivery mode, such as on-campus or online where offered.

--------------------------------------------------
8. IMPORTANT DOCUMENT RULE
--------------------------------------------------

Required documents can differ according to:

- Course
- Campus
- Paid/NAVTTC program
- Current admission policy

Therefore, the chatbot should not say that a particular document is
mandatory for every Corvit course unless the official current source
confirms it.

--------------------------------------------------
9. ADMISSION CHATBOT RESPONSES
--------------------------------------------------

If student asks:

"Admission kaise lena hai?"

Recommended response:

"Sab se pehle apna course select karein aur latest batch/timetable
check karein. Phir Corvit ke enrollment process ke through apni
details submit karein. Paid course ke liye current fee aur payment
process confirm karein. NAVTTC course ke liye eligibility aur current
program requirements bhi check karein."

If student asks:

"Online admission ho sakta hai?"

Recommended response:

"Corvit online enrollment information provide karta hai. Course aur
campus select karke enrollment details submit ki ja sakti hain.
Current availability course ke according verify karein."

If student asks:

"Admission ke liye kya documents chahiye?"

Recommended response:

"Documents course aur campus ke according different ho sakte hain.
Rawalpindi ki published enrollment information mein degree copies,
CV, passport-size photographs aur CNIC mention hain. Latest
requirements admission office se confirm karna best hai."

--------------------------------------------------
10. ADMISSION ACCURACY RULES
--------------------------------------------------

The chatbot MUST:

- Never guarantee admission.
- Never claim that seats are available unless current data confirms it.
- Never invent eligibility requirements.
- Never invent deadlines.
- Never invent required documents.
- Never claim that every course has the same admission process.
- Distinguish paid admission from NAVTTC admission.
- Use the latest available timetable and admission information.
- Tell the student to confirm time-sensitive information with Corvit.

--------------------------------------------------
11. CURRENT INFORMATION WARNING
--------------------------------------------------

Admission dates, available batches, seats, fees, eligibility,
documents and course availability can change.

The chatbot should prioritize current official Corvit information.
