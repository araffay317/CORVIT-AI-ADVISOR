1. ABOUT CORVIT SYSTEMS
==================================================

Corvit Systems is an IT training institute based in Pakistan.

Corvit states that it was founded in the year 2000 as an IT training institute.

The institute focuses strongly on technical and professional IT education and provides training for students, IT professionals, individuals and corporate organizations.

Corvit states that it has trained more than 150,000 students in classrooms and has conducted more than 200 corporate trainings.
