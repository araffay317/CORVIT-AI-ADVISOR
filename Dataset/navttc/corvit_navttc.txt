==================================================
1. WHAT IS NAVTTC?
==================================================

NAVTTC stands for National Vocational and Technical
Training Commission.

NAVTTC is a Government of Pakistan organization
responsible for promoting and supporting technical and
vocational education and training.

Corvit Systems describes itself as a NAVTTC-approved
training institute.


==================================================
2. NAVTTC TRAINING AT CORVIT
==================================================

Corvit Systems offers NAVTTC-supported technical and
vocational training programs.

Corvit states that its NAVTTC programs are funded by
the Government of Pakistan.

For eligible students, these programs are offered
without tuition fees.

The availability of a particular program can depend
on the current NAVTTC project, batch and seats.

5. NAVTTC CERTIFICATION
==================================================

Corvit states that students who successfully complete
the relevant NAVTTC program receive an official NAVTTC
certificate.

The certificate is described by Corvit as being
recognized across Pakistan.


==================================================
6. GENERAL ELIGIBILITY
==================================================

Eligibility requirements can vary according to the
specific NAVTTC program and current NAVTTC project.

Corvit's current NAVTTC page states that Pakistani
students who meet the basic education and age
requirements set by NAVTTC can apply.

Corvit also states that most courses require at least
Intermediate education.

IMPORTANT:

The chatbot must NOT tell every student that the same
eligibility criteria apply to every course.

Instead, it should say:

"Eligibility can vary by course and current NAVTTC
requirements. Please check the latest eligibility
requirements for the specific course."

==================================================
10. NAVTTC VS PAID TRAINING
==================================================

NAVTTC TRAINING:

- Government-supported/funded program
- Free tuition for eligible students
- Eligibility requirements apply
- Course availability depends on current NAVTTC
  programs/batches
- NAVTTC certification may be provided after
  successful completion
- Seats may be limited


PAID TRAINING:

- Student pays the course fee
- Course availability depends on Corvit's current
  paid-course offerings
- Fee depends on the selected course
- Schedule depends on the current batch
- Certification depends on the specific course/program

13. IMPORTANT CHATBOT RULES
==================================================

RULE 1:
Never tell a student that a NAVTTC course is available
unless it exists in the latest dataset.

checking the relevant course requirements.
